YOUR PHOTOS GO HERE  (Ch.01 · the flip-book)
============================================

There are 12 PLACEHOLDER images already in this folder:
   photo-01.jpg  ...  photo-12.jpg

EASIEST WAY (no code editing):
  Just REPLACE any of these files with your own image, and KEEP THE SAME NAME.
  e.g. delete photo-01.jpg and drop in your own file renamed to  photo-01.jpg
  The book will show your photo automatically.

Tips:
  - Portrait or square photos look best (each page is a 3:4 card).
  - jpg / png / webp all work — just keep the filename the same.

WANT DIFFERENT FILENAMES OR CAPTIONS?
  Open  src/experience/Experience.jsx  → block marked "ADD YOUR PHOTOS HERE"
  (the PHOTO_TILES array). For each page you can change:
     img   -> "/photos/whatever.jpg"   (or "" to show the plain gradient page)
     quote -> the line shown on the LEFT of the section for that page
     note  -> handwritten caption on the FRONT of the page
     back  -> short note on the BACK of the page
  Add more entries and the book grows (a cover + "The End" page are auto-added).
